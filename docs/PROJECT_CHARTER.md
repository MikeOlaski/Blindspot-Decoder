# Project Charter

## Product
Blind Spot Decoder is a browser-based assessment experience that reveals communication and behavior blind spots, then converts insight into lead capture and premium follow-up.

## Current north-star outcomes
- Deliver actionable insight in under 3 minutes.
- Convert qualified users via full-report email gate.
- Maintain trust with clear non-clinical framing and privacy constraints.

## In-scope (current)
- Next.js web app.
- Prompted assessment flow (text + optional multimodal capture).
- Structured analysis response.
- Lead capture and email report delivery.

## Out-of-scope (current)
- Clinical diagnosis.
- Team accounts and multi-user collaboration.
- Enterprise-grade CRM orchestration.

## Decision principles
- Preserve speed over feature sprawl.
- Keep AI claims grounded to what is actually measured.
- Treat blind spots as overused strengths, never shame language.
- Prefer server-side secrets and server actions for all third-party integrations.
