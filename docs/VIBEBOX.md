# VIBEBOX

Purpose: preserve product feel and narrative consistency across UI, copy, and interaction patterns.

## Voice
- Perceptive, warm, direct, credible.
- Never clinical, never shaming.
- Frame blind spots as overused strengths.

## UX posture
- Keep flow short and high-signal.
- Use progress and momentum cues.
- Avoid dense wall-of-text screens.

## Result language rules
- Always include: pattern, impact, leverage, first shift.
- Avoid deterministic labels like "you are X".
- Prefer "you may be experiencing" framing.

## Conversion posture
- Insight first, gate second.
- Gate copy must feel like continuation, not interruption.
- CTA language should stay practical and non-hyped.
